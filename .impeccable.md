## Design Context

### Users
Visitors to John Marlett's personal site: peers, collaborators, curious strangers. They arrive with no task to complete. In about ten seconds they should know what John is about, and then do one of three things: read the blog, find a way to get in touch, or simply enjoy a small moment of delight (the background, the Lab experiments). The site showcases John's creative interests (software, AI agents, sailing simulation, browser experiments). It is not a sales funnel.

### Brand Personality
- **Three words:** Futuristic, Retro, Classical.
- **Core image:** the Venus de Milo in the space age. Classical sculpture seen through a terminal: marble meets phosphor.
- **Voice:** spare and confident. Let the work and visuals speak. No marketing copy, no "Welcome to my website", no AI-helper-style explanatory filler. Short labels, short sentences.
- **Emotion:** quiet intrigue and calm curiosity, with a small surprise when you look closer.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme:** dark only.
- **Composition:** one centered visual surrounded by generous negative space. Calm and symmetrical, like a statue in a gallery.
- **Background:** a pixel animation (dot or pixel grid, dither, faint glyph rain), subtle and slow and *almost* Matrix-like. It is texture, never spectacle, and must never compete with content.
- **Imagery:** the Venus de Milo stays as the central mark, rendered in a space-age way (pixelated, dithered, scanline, or point-cloud), not as a photo. The Milky Way hero photo will be removed.
- **Palette:** near-black ground and marble or bone-white type. Color is restrained: at most one muted, low-saturation accent used sparingly (a phosphor-like tint). No bright color swatches or saturated blocks.
- **Typography:** pair a classical serif (Roman inscription feel) with a monospace for labels and metadata. Avoid generic geometric startup sans for display type.
- **Reference:** sybilsolutions.ai (personal site with a technical, dark, AI-native feel). Its stylesheet couldn't be read from a fetch, so check it in the browser during the design pass.
- **Anti-references:** Squarespace/startup templates (full-bleed stock hero, "Engineering Excellence" taglines, pill CTAs, fade-up-everything), skeuomorphism (bevels, glossy textures, fake materials), bright color swatches, and verbose AI-helper copy.

### Design Principles
1. **Statue in the void.** One centered focal element, lots of negative space. If something doesn't earn its place, remove it.
2. **Texture, not noise.** Pixel and glyph motion lives in the background at low contrast and slow tempo. Content stays crisp and static.
3. **Marble and phosphor.** Classical form (serif, symmetry, proportion) rendered with retro-digital means (pixels, mono, scanlines). Both must be present. Neither should become kitsch.
4. **Say less.** Minimal copy, no helper text. Labels over paragraphs. Delight comes from discovery, not explanation.
5. **Basic accessibility is non-negotiable.** WCAG AA text contrast, visible focus states, keyboard navigable, and all background animation paused or reduced under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
