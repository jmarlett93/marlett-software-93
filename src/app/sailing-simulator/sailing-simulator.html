Boat speed comes from the official Melges 24 polar — a table of boat speed in knots for each wind speed and wind angle. Polars encode how fast a boat can go close-hauled, reaching, and running; the physics engine moves the boat according to these values, and the auto pilot uses them to plan tacks and mark approaches. Browse published polars at for more information. boatpolars.com.

I used PIXI JS and web GL for rendering. In the future I will add additional boats to race against, enhance the physics engine to include acceleration, enhance weather by building in weather model patterns, include different strategies for the auto pilot to predict best course based on weather pattern recognition over time, and finally a tactical module so the auto pilot makes specific calls to beat other boats based on predicted positioning, race rules and weather predictions.
